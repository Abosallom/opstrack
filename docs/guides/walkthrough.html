The first minute

Does it answer your question?

1 Open nphiescore.com on your phone. “82 of 406 capabilities live”, across 104 organizations, with three bars under it: Live, In testing, Planned. ok / not
2 Scroll down that first screen. Ten capability rows, then the ladder, then what moved recently. No sideways scrolling anywhere. ok / not
3 Press العربية in the header. Everything flips to Arabic and right-to-left, on the same screen, without reloading. Press it again to come back. ok / not
4 Tap Map. A list of organizations, not a picture. That is deliberate on a phone: the tree gives 104 organizations fourteen pixels each. ok / not
5 Press the Map button in the bar above the list. The picture appears. The button now reads Table. Press it again and you are back to the list — and it remembers which you chose. ok / not

The things a member does every day

Can you actually use it?

6 On the map, tap the magnifying glass in the header and type Hamra. Al Hamra Hospital appears as you type. Press it and the map opens there. ok / not
7 Tap any organization. A panel opens from the side with its details — stage, owner, capabilities — and the fields can be edited. ok / not
8 Type one line into the box at the bottom of the map and press Capture. Try: test line #UHR !high The tokens turn into fields as you type. After Capture, the item exists. Delete it afterwards — this is a test. ok / not
9 Turn on flight mode, capture another line, then turn flight mode off. It says it is saved on your device, a banner counts it as waiting, and it syncs by itself when the network returns. Nothing you typed is lost. ok / not
10 Tap PMO, then swipe along its tabs. Eight tabs. Most are empty and say so plainly rather than showing a zero. The last reads الأهداف والنتائج (OKR) in Arabic. ok / not
