Check your work

Two ways to do this. Pick whichever you prefer — they answer the same question.

The easy way — in a browser

Go to dnschecker.org, type nphiescore.com in the box, choose A from the dropdown, and press search. You are looking for the four numbers ending 108, 109, 110, 111. Then change the dropdown to AAAA, and finally to CNAME with www.nphiescore.com in the box.

The precise way — in Terminal

Press ⌘ + Space, type Terminal, press Enter. A window with text in it opens. Paste these one line at a time, pressing Enter after each:

$ dig +short nphiescore.com A 185.199.108.153 185.199.109.153 185.199.110.153 185.199.111.153 $ dig +short nphiescore.com AAAA 2606:50c0:8000::153 (and the other three) $ dig +short www.nphiescore.com CNAME abosallom.github.io.

The order the lines come back in does not matter — it shuffles deliberately. What matters is the count: four, four, and one.

If you get nothing back, or still see 2.57.91.91

Nothing is wrong. Other computers were told to remember the old answer for four hours, and that time has not run out yet. Make a cup of tea, or leave it until tomorrow, and run it again. There is nothing to fix and no way to hurry it.

https://nphiescore.com will not show your app yet, and that is correct. GitHub does not know about the domain until I publish the app to it, which is a later step and mine. Expect an error page. The checks above are the whole test.
