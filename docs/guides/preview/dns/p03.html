The one thing to know: there will be a gap where nothing works

Between page 5 (where you delete the old entries) and page 8 (where you finish adding the new ones), nphiescore.com answers nothing at all. That is normal and expected. Nobody is using the address, so nobody notices. Do not stop in the middle and worry — just carry on to the end of page 8.
