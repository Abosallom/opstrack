BRD-001 Patient-data quarantine built
‏حَجْر بيانات المرضى‏

Ticket descriptions, comments, attachments, environment and worklog are never fetched, quoted or re-derived: jira-read refuses those field names outright and refuses the *all / *navigable wildcards that would fetch them without naming them, and every measurement in the programme is taken from the safe projection. Cleaning the source field is separate, later work with its own owner. If a question can only be answered by reading Description, the answer is that the question is not answered yet.

‏وصف التذكرة وتعليقاتها ومرفقاتها وحقل environment وسجل العمل لا تُجلب ولا تُقتبس ولا يُعاد اشتقاقها أبدًا: دالة jira-read ترفض أسماء هذه الحقول صراحةً، وترفض معها ‎*all‎ و‎*navigable‎ اللذين يجرّانها دون تسميتها، وكل قياس في البرنامج يُؤخذ من الإسقاط الآمن. أما تنظيف الحقل نفسه في المصدر فعمل منفصل لاحق وله مسؤوله. وإذا كان السؤال لا يُجاب إلا بقراءة Description، فالجواب أن السؤال لم يُجب بعد.‏

Measured — the scan of the 2,971-ticket export found raw HL7 v2 clinical payloads pasted into Descriptions: 522 IPv4 addresses, 37 ten-digit national-ID/Iqama-shaped numbers, 22 MRN or patient-id mentions, 9 certificate or private-key blocks, 57 email addresses. The safe projection keeps 628 of 702 columns over the same 2,971 rows and was verified before use — HL7 segment markers 8 → 0, key blocks 24 → 0, emails 197 → 1.
BRD-023 The CHI contact is a name and nothing else agreed
‏جهة اتصال CHI اسم ولا شيء غيره‏

coc_contact holds a person’s name. No email, no phone, no title. A contact outside the organization is a higher bar than a colleague, not a lower one; contact details stay in whatever system the PMO already keeps them in.

‏coc_contact يحمل اسم شخص. لا بريد، ولا هاتف، ولا مسمّى وظيفي. فجهة اتصال خارج المنظمة سقفها أعلى من سقف الزميل لا أدنى، وتفاصيل التواصل تبقى في النظام الذي يحفظها فيه الـ PMO أصلًا.‏

Measured — this workspace holds no staff emails by design and forbids attachments outright, and its privacy page is written from what the schema actually contains. The safe projection had to reduce 197 email addresses to 1 before anything could be measured at all.
BRD-034 The weekly report carries its denominators agreed
‏التقرير الأسبوعي يحمل مقاماته‏

The compliance report runs fourteen rules, resolves each to a person (the Assignee, then the organization’s Account Manager, then the PMO Lead — and a rule that resolves to nobody is a finding in its own right), prints every sentence against its denominator and never as a bare percentage, and goes out in Arabic and English at Sunday 07:00 Asia/Riyadh.

‏تقرير الالتزام يشغّل أربع عشرة قاعدة، ويحيل كل قاعدة إلى شخص (المُسنَد إليه، ثم Account Manager الخاص بالمنظمة، ثم قائد الـ PMO — والقاعدة التي لا تُسنَد إلى أحد ملاحظة بذاتها)، ويطبع كل جملة على مقامها لا نسبةً مجرّدة، ويخرج بالعربية والإنجليزية الأحد الساعة 07:00 بتوقيت الرياض.‏

Measured — its first run reads 497 of 1,551 pairs with a live record, 179 with only a closed one and 875 never written; 531 of 552 open onboarding tickets name no rung, 552 of 552 carry no due date, 550 of 552 have no checklist and 435 of 552 are held by four people.  Overleaf: what it looks like.
