Check the names first

Part one of two. This changes nothing — it only shows you what it intends to do.

Every person gets a username, worked out from their name. A username is what they type at every single sign-in, and it cannot be changed later without locking them out of their own account. So you read the list before anything is created.

1
In Terminal, paste these two lines:
cd /Users/aziz/Claude/nphiescore node scripts/provision-people.mjs
A table of eighteen names appears. Nothing is written — it says “dry run” at the top.
2
Read every row of the “Signs in as” column. These are permanent. Look especially at the ones where the spelling could reasonably go more than one way — sara.alqhahtani is the obvious one.
3
If any is wrong, tell me and I will correct it before you go on. Fixing it now is a one-line change; fixing it after the account exists is not fixable at all.

Two rows will say EXISTS — that is correct

Yours and Nasser's. The script recognises you both and deliberately refuses to create second accounts for you, because a second identity would split your history in half and Nasser's username is his login. So: eighteen names on the list, sixteen new accounts.

You can run this as many times as you like

It writes nothing, needs no password, and can be run today, tomorrow, and again after a correction. It is purely a preview.
