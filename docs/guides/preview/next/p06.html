1

2
Set Site URL to https://nphiescore.com
3
Add to the Redirect URLs list: https://nphiescore.com/** — and leave the two that are already there. Three entries in the list, not one.

Keep the old entry for a week or two

It costs nothing to leave, and removing it early strands anyone who is halfway through signing in on a link they were sent yesterday. Take it out later, once nobody is using the old address.
