What I am building between your sittings

So the wait makes sense. This came out of benchmarking the tools a programme director will have used — Asana, Smartsheet, Wrike, Jira Align, Miro, XMind — against what you have.

The map itself

A vertical tree with real connectors and real names, that opens on the whole programme. It collapses, it searches, and it has a settings screen with a live preview.

Memory — the biggest gap, and it is invisible

Right now the tool records where each organization is, but not where it has been. So it cannot answer “are we moving faster than last month”, “how long does Testing actually take”, or “will we hit December” — even though your target dates are already stored. I am adding that memory. It starts recording the day it ships, so the sooner it lands the sooner the answers exist.

The three things a director would notice were missing

Search — typing a hospital's name and finding it on the map. A stated red/amber/green with a reason, beside the one the clock works out, so a manager who knows better can say so. A go-live forecast — the line of organizations live against your plan, and whether you will make the date.
