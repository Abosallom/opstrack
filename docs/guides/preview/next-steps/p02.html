A one-time password that turns an account from “created” into “usable”. The person types it once, chooses their own password, and the code dies. It is shown once and can never be shown again — only replaced.

The one-page screen where a person does that, at nphiescore.com/#/claim. They need their username, their code, and a password they invent on the spot.
The portfolio
The PMO tab’s new half: your projects, initiatives, actions, risks, revenue and OKRs. It went live today with the forms to fill it. It is empty.
Capability
One of the ten things an organization can integrate — eligibility, pre-authorization, claims and so on. Each organization’s ten make the 406 links.

The one rule

Invite codes are handed over in person, never emailed and never typed into a chat — including mine. They are passwords. The app itself says so on the screen.
