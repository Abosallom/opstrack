What it answers

What one organisation looks like, whole: owner, rung and all 10 capabilities on a single object you can point at in a meeting. It is the only view where an empty ring — nobody has said anything — sits beside a grey planned dot at readable size.

What it hides

79 organisations are not on the page; the sample stands in for them. Nothing here adds up a column, so you cannot read which capability is furthest ahead, and a card cannot say when a cell was last touched or how long its rung has held.
