Thirty-seven requirements for tracking the onboarding of Saudi Arabia’s hospitals onto the Unified Health Record. Each one carries its English and its Arabic side by side in this file, and each one names the measurement it came from.

The change, in one line

The unit of record becomes one ticket per hospital per use case — 141 × 11 = 1,551 — and the rung it has reached becomes its status, so that what nobody has said stays visibly unsaid.

Who approves

Abdulaziz Alsaloom, PMO Lead, and nobody else. Nothing here is built before that mark is on page 28. A job title never carries approval.

It commits no dates. 89 of the 2,971 tickets in the tracker carry a due date; 3 of the 828 open ones do; 0 of the 552 open onboarding ones do. Asking people for dates has been tried here. BRD-005 A record closes only from PROD, only with a reason agreed
‏السجل لا يُغلق إلا من PROD وبسبب مذكور‏

An Onboarding record may be closed only from PROD, only when all six PROD conditions are met, and only with a Resolution that says why: Live, Merged into duplicate, Superseded, Not applicable, Withdrawn. PROD stays in the In Progress category because a cutover is not done; Closed is the single terminal state and withdrawal is a Resolution, not a status. Nothing is ever closed for inactivity, to clear a queue, or closed-and-reraised.

‏لا يُغلق سجل الـ onboarding إلا من PROD، وبعد تحقق شروط PROD الستة كلها، وبـ Resolution يقول لماذا: Live أو Merged into duplicate أو Superseded أو Not applicable أو Withdrawn. وتبقى PROD ضمن فئة In Progress لأن الـ cutover ليس انتهاءً، وClosed هي الحالة النهائية الوحيدة، والانسحاب Resolution لا حالة. ولا يُغلق شيء لركوده، ولا لتفريغ طابور، ولا يُغلق ثم يُعاد رفعه من جديد.‏
