And what I am deliberately not building

Four things from the PMO document, refused

A council of three reviewed the whole request before I started. These four came back as no, and I would rather tell you why than quietly drop them.

Open-by-link access

The original dashboard is readable and editable by anyone with the link. In this app, a request with no signed-in user is treated as the most trusted writer there is — that is how the internal jobs are recognised. Opening a public door would quietly hand every stranger the highest level of trust in the system. This one is not a preference.

Revenue, and the document store

There is no money anywhere in this system and nothing to check a figure against; a revenue tab would be numbers nobody can verify. And the app stores no files at all — your privacy policy says so. The seven documents work as a checklist with a link each: same progress bar, no filing cabinet.

PowerPoint and PDF export

Both need new libraries this app has deliberately never taken on — it draws its own charts for that reason. Your browser already prints to PDF, and the Digest already produces a report you can paste straight into Word or Outlook with its formatting intact.

The 176 SGH activities — not refused, but not yet

Making them editable here would build a second task tracker inside an app whose whole story is that onboarding lives in Jira. Within a month there would be two versions of the truth and you would trust neither. If the plan matters, it belongs where the work is, and this tool should read it. Say the word if you disagree — it is your call, not mine.

The short version

Put day numbers on four stages. That is the only thing standing between you and a quarter of the app. Everything else is mine or can wait.
