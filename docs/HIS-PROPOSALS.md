# HIS — what the data can and cannot tell us

**Nothing here is written to the database.** Every line is a proposal for a person to confirm.

## ⚠ The headline, and it corrects an earlier estimate

I told the owner that "roughly 60 to 80 of 161 are recoverable from strings you already hold".
**That was wrong.** The measurement says **16**. Widening the matcher — the same word-boundary fix
that took the org-rulings sheet from 18 unmatched to 1 — moved it from 15 to 16, so this is not a
matcher artefact. It is the finding:

> **The data does not carry the HIS for 142 of 161 hospitals.**

Which changes what this task is. It is not an extraction job with a manual tail. It is **a
data-collection task with a small head start** — one field, per hospital, from the OB Account
Manager who already owns it.

## ⚠ Rhapsody is deliberately not in the catalogue

It is named 93 times in ticket text and would top any frequency ranking. It is the **integration
engine our own technical team builds interfaces in** — the owner's words: *"requesting the
technical team to build the interface in rhapsody to the needed hospital"*. It is our tool, not a
property of a hospital. Including it would tag most of the estate with one meaningless value and
make the HIS axis useless on the day it shipped.

## And one thing this measurement settled

`Aseer` and `Aseer Cluster` — the two rows left **pending allocation** when Aseer was split by
system — lean **Careware 12 mentions to Vida Plus 2** in ticket text. That is evidence, not a
ruling, but it is the evidence the allocation was waiting for.
